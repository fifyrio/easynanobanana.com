> Hi Product Hunt! 👋
>
> I built Easy Nano Banana after watching friends struggle with the same thing: they knew exactly what they wanted changed in a photo, but not how to do it in an editor. When Google's Nano Banana image model came out, I realized the interface could just be… a sentence.
>
> So each tool on the site does one job: remove an object, swap an outfit, turn a selfie into a Minecraft skin, colorize a family photo. Behind all of them is the same idea — describe the change, get the image.
>
> Honest limitation: complex multi-step edits (e.g. "move this person AND relight the scene AND change the season") still work better as separate passes than one prompt. I'm working on chained edits next.
